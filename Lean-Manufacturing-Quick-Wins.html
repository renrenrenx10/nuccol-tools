Quick wins for small teams

Lean Manufacturing

You don't need a lean transformation programme to get real results. Five small, practical changes you can start this week.

5 min readOperations

01Run a 5S pass on one area

Sort, straighten, shine, standardise, sustain — pick one workbench or cell and just do it. Start this week.

02Put up a simple visual KPI board

One whiteboard, three or four numbers that matter (output, quality, on-time delivery). Start this week.

03Write SOPs for your top 3 recurring problems

Not everything — just the three issues that cost you the most time. Start this week.

04Use a simple root-cause method

Ask 'why' five times on your next defect or delay before acting. Start this week.

05Hold a 10-minute daily huddle

Same time, same three questions: what went well, what didn't, what needs help. Start this week.

06Why this matters for F4N and CSIP

These are exactly the kind of practical improvements an F4N assessment and a Continuous Improvement Plan (CSIP) look for — small, evidenced changes that compound over time, not a big-bang transformation.
