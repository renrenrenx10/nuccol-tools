Section 1
Executive Summary

Example Fabrication Ltd holds F4N certification (granted 2025) and a strong Quality & Compliance rating under SCRL. Core capability — CNC precision machining and sub-assembly — aligns closely with near-term demand from two SMR developers and one large-scale new build programme.

The clearest opportunity in the next 12 months sits in SMR developer demand and GW new build, where indicative near-term needs match current capability with only moderate capacity investment required. Opportunities in STEP Fusion, hydrogen and CCUS are earlier-stage and better suited to a 2–3 year positioning horizon.

The single largest gap identified is ISO 19443 certification, not yet in place. Closing this gap is the highest-leverage action available in the next two quarters, and is addressed in the 90-day action plan (Section 12).

Section 10
Competitive Positioning & Recommendations

Strengths: strong F4N and quality track record, precision machining depth, established relationships with two SMR developer supply chain teams.

Watch-outs: single-shift capacity constrains volume commitments; ISO 19443 gap will exclude Tier 2 GW new build packages until closed.

  • Prioritise SMR developer engagement (Section 3) and GW new build ISO 19443 readiness (Section 4) as the primary 12-month focus.
  • Treat STEP Fusion, hydrogen and CCUS as 2–3 year diversification options, not immediate priorities.
  • Defer offshore wind unless capacity becomes genuinely spare — margin profile is less favourable than core nuclear work.
