About this self-assessment

This is based on the seven categories F4N (Fit For Nuclear) uses to assess UK manufacturers: Nuclear Industry Fundamentals, Nuclear Safety Culture, Quality & Compliance, Leadership & Governance, Human Performance, Supply Chain Readiness, and Security of Information. Each question below has a short note explaining why it matters — read it even if you're unsure how to answer, since understanding the "why" is often more useful than the tick itself. There's no pass mark and nobody sees your answers but you; it exists purely to help you have an honest conversation with yourself (and your team) about where you actually stand today.

What to do with your result
  • Don't treat a low score as bad news — it simply tells you where a formal F4N assessment is likely to add the most value first.
  • Share this with a colleague or two and compare answers — differences between how people at different levels of the business see things are often the most useful finding of all.
  • Use the category names above to jump to the matching guide on the Best Practice Hub for a deeper dive into any area where you scored lower than expected.
  • Revisit this in three to six months — readiness is something you build over time, and tracking change is more useful than a single snapshot.
