The Programme

What is F4N?

NuclearReady is the directory of UK manufacturers currently undertaking or granted F4N (Fit For Nuclear) status under NucCol.

F4N is a structured programme that prepares UK manufacturers to compete in the nuclear sector — helping companies understand what nuclear buyers expect, identify gaps, and meet the requirements to win work.

Assessment is carried out by NucCol's supply chain consultants, who visit each company in person, verify a self-assessment across six categories of business excellence, and work with them to build an action plan against nuclear industry standards.

Companies working through this process are listed as On the Journey. Those approved by the F4N granting panel earn F4N Granted status — the recognised mark that a business is ready to compete for nuclear contracts.

01
Capability Questionnaire & Self-Assessment
An initial questionnaire and online self-assessment covering six categories of business operation establish a clear baseline before consultant involvement.
02
Site Verification & Action Plan
NucCol's supply chain consultants visit your premises, verify the self-assessment, and co-create a structured action plan to close gaps against nuclear buyer expectations.
03
Sector Knowledge & Development
Nuclear-specific knowledge assessments and online knowledge transfer modules build your team's understanding of procurement standards and technical requirements.
04
Pre-Grant Visit & F4N Granted
After completing your action plans, a final pre-grant visit leads to assessment by the granting panel. Granted companies are listed in this directory and gain full NucCol membership benefits.
