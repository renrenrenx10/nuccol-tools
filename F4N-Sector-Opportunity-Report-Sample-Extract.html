01What a Sector Opportunity Report is for

A Sector Opportunity Report exists to translate broad sector-level demand data into something specific and actionable for one company — rather than leaving you to interpret generic market reports yourself, it maps demand directly against your own capability data and highlights the gaps worth closing first.

02Executive summary

This sample shows how a Sector Opportunity Report translates SMR (Small Modular Reactor) developer demand and your own capability data into a focused, practical action plan, rather than a generic overview of the sector as a whole.

06How to actually use a report like this

The value of a Sector Opportunity Report isn't in reading it once — it's in using the gap summary as a genuine input to your own business planning, and revisiting the action plan quarterly to check progress against it, updating it as your own capability and the sector's demand both evolve.
