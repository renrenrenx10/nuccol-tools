Export Controls & Customs for Nuclear Exporters

The key concepts you need to know before exporting nuclear-relevant goods or technology.

6 min readLegal & Compliance
This guide is general information, not legal advice. Always seek specific guidance for your own situation.

01Key concepts

TermWhat it means
Dual-use itemsGoods or technology with both civilian and military/strategic applications
Strategic export controlsUK rules restricting export of sensitive goods, software and technology
Commodity codesCodes used to classify goods for customs and control purposes
Sanctions regimesRestrictions on trade with specific countries, entities or individuals
SPIREThe UK's system for applying for export licences
AEOAuthorised Economic Operator — a trusted-trader customs status

02A sensible starting checklist

  • Check whether what you're exporting could be classed as dual-use or nuclear-related.
  • Screen your customer and destination country against current sanctions lists.
  • Confirm the correct commodity code before shipping.
  • Establish whether an export licence is required before you commit to a contract.
